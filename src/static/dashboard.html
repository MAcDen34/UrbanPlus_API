Fitted history and projection

Solid line is fitted to observed data. The shaded cone is the model’s margin of error, which grows by about 1.1 percentage points for every year past 2020.

Relative growth since 2020

Shading shows projected growth against each district’s 2020 figure. Select a district to chart it.

The margin of error is extrapolated from drift measured on a 2016–2020 holdout and a single 2022 census check. It is an indicative band, not a statistical confidence interval. Projections far beyond 2020 should be read as direction and scale, not as counts.
